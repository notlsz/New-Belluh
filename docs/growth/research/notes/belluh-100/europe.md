## 1. Dominant messaging app per country (which channel should Bella live on?)

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is the default channel for Bella in almost all of Western and Southern Europe. The exceptions that matter: the Nordics (Messenger and Snapchat are strong in SE, NO and DK), the Balkans and Eastern Europe (Viber in BG, GR, RS, HU and partly HR; Telegram and Viber in Ukraine), and the UK, where iMessage is a real second channel because of high iPhone share.

### Cited Findings
- Top app per GWI/DataReportal Digital 2025 data, as republished by Sinch (published 7 Nov 2025, updated 4 Mar 2026; leader only, no percentages) **[V]** — [Sinch](https://sinch.com/blog/most-popular-messaging-apps-by-country/):
  - **WhatsApp:** Austria, Belgium, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Portugal, Spain, Switzerland, UK
  - **Messenger:** Denmark, Norway, Sweden
  - **Telegram:** Finland. Treat as **suspect**: earlier DataReportal Finland reports show WhatsApp as the top messenger **[bg]** ([DataReportal Finland](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-finland)). Conflict unresolved.
- Viber is the leading or co-leading messenger in Bulgaria, Greece, Serbia, Hungary (alongside Messenger) and Ukraine (alongside Telegram) **[bg]** — [DataReportal country reports](https://datareportal.com/reports/)
- In Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, Romania and Hungary, Messenger and WhatsApp are close **[bg]** — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/)
- iOS share is high in the UK, Nordics, Switzerland and Denmark (roughly 45-55%) but low in Southern and Eastern Europe (about 20-30%). This decides whether iMessage is worth supporting **[bg]** — [StatCounter mobile OS Europe](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/europe)

### Inferences
- **Launch order:** WhatsApp Business Platform first across the EU, then Messenger for the Nordics, Viber for BG/GR/RS/HU/UA, Telegram for UA, and iMessage (Apple Messages for Business) for the UK, CH and Nordics.
- **Pricing risk:** WhatsApp Business charges per template message, with rates that vary by country. Proactive "anniversary in 3 days" nudges count as utility or marketing templates, and Western Europe has some of the highest rates (Germany, France, Netherlands). Model this cost before setting the Duo price.

### Gaps
- No country-level penetration percentages: the DataReportal fetch was blocked.
- The Finland Telegram claim is unconfirmed.

## 3. Romantic calendar and customs

### Takeaway
Feb 14 is universal but varies in weight:
- Big in UK, FR, IT, ES, PL, RO.
- Weaker in DE, AT and the Nordics.
- "Friendship day" in Finland and Estonia.
- Competes with Trifon Zarezan (wine saint) in Bulgaria and Serbia.

Bella needs a per-country event table that includes name days in Central and Eastern Europe and Greece.

### Cited Findings (all **[bg]**; day verified against [timeanddate.com holidays](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/) where noted)
- **UK:**
  - Valentine's Day (Feb 14)
  - Wales: Dydd Santes Dwynwen (Jan 25), the Welsh patron saint of lovers — [timeanddate](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/uk/st-dwynwens-day)
  - Leap-day proposals (Feb 29, an Irish and Scottish tradition)
  - Mothering Sunday (3 weeks before Easter)
  - Christmas and New Year's Eve are peak engagement dates
- **Spain:**
  - San Valentín (Feb 14)
  - Catalonia: Sant Jordi (Apr 23). Men traditionally give a rose and women a book; today both give both. A major flower and book commerce day — [timeanddate](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/spain/st-george-day)
  - Valencia: Sant Donís (Oct 9), when men give marzipan "mocadorà" sweets
- **Italy:**
  - San Valentino (Feb 14)
  - Festa della Donna (Mar 8), with mimosa sprigs
  - Love padlocks; anniversary dinners
- **France:**
  - Saint-Valentin (Feb 14)
  - Muguet (lily of the valley) on May 1, given for luck
  - Fête des Mères (late May or June)
- **Germany and Austria:**
  - Valentinstag is modest (flowers and dinner)
  - Named wedding anniversaries: Papierhochzeit (1), Hölzerne (5), Rosenhochzeit (10), Silberne (25), Goldene (50)
- **Netherlands and Belgium:**
  - Valentijnsdag, with anonymous cards
  - In Belgium, Mother's Day differs in Antwerp (Aug 15)
- **Nordics:**
  - Alla hjärtans dag (SE), Valentinsdag (NO, DK)
  - Denmark: gækkebrev, anonymous snowdrop letters before Easter
  - Finland: Ystävänpäivä (Feb 14) is friend's day; name days (nimipäivä) are on the official calendar
  - Midsummer (Juhannus, Midsommar, Sankthans) is a key romantic and couple occasion
- **Iceland:** Bóndadagur (first day of Þorri, late Jan, gifts to husband or boyfriend) and Konudagur (first day of Góa, late Feb, flowers to wife or girlfriend)
- **Czechia:** Lovers' Day on May 1, when couples kiss under a blossoming cherry tree; people visit the Mácha statue on Petřín Hill. Svátek (name days) is widely celebrated.
- **Slovakia:** also kisses under the cherry tree on May 1; name days are widely celebrated
- **Poland:**
  - Walentynki (Feb 14)
  - Andrzejki (Nov 29-30), a love fortune-telling night
  - Imieniny (name days) are often bigger than birthdays among older generations
  - Noc Kupały (Jun 23-24)
- **Romania:**
  - Dragobete (Feb 24), a native lovers' day
  - Mărțișor (Mar 1), red-and-white charms given to women
  - Name days (onomastica)
- **Moldova:** also observes Mărțișor and Dragobete (Moldova is out of scope)
- **Bulgaria:** Trifon Zarezan (Feb 14, vine-pruning wine day) versus Valentine's; Baba Marta and martenitsa (Mar 1); name days (imen den)
- **Ukraine:** Ivan Kupala (night of Jul 6-7; also Jun 23-24 after the 2023 calendar reform), with flower wreaths on water and searching for the fern flower; Valentine's widely marked
- **Baltics:** Jāņi/Līgo (LV, Jun 23-24), Jaanipäev (EE), Joninės (LT); Estonia's Feb 14 sõbrapäev is also "friend's day"; Latvia and Lithuania observe name days
- **Greece and Cyprus:** name days (onomastiki giorti) often matter more than birthdays; Agios Valentinos (Feb 14)
- **Portugal:**
  - Dia dos Namorados (Feb 14)
  - Santo António (Jun 12-13, Lisbon matchmaker saint), with manjericos (basil pots with love quatrains) and the city-funded Santo António weddings
  - Minho lenços dos namorados (embroidered love handkerchiefs)
- **Slovenia:** Gregorjevo (Mar 12, "birds' wedding"), an old lovers' day
- **Croatia:** Licitar gingerbread hearts as love tokens (UNESCO-listed)
- **Hungary, Croatia, Slovenia:** name days (névnap, imendan) are common

### Inferences
- Seed Bella's calendar per country: national romantic days, plus name days (CZ, SK, PL, HU, HR, SI, BG, GR, CY, RO, LV, LT, FI, SE, plus AT/DE Catholic regions), plus Mother's Day dates, which vary by country.
- Name-day data can be derived from public calendars. Bella asks for both partners' names at onboarding and auto-adds the name days.

## 4. Couple culture: cohabitation, marriage, religiosity, privacy, LGBTQ+ status

### Takeaway
Marriage is late and often optional. The Nordics, France and the Benelux are cohabitation-first (PACS in FR, sambo in SE), with mean age at first marriage around 33-37. Southern Europe marries late as well (ES about 37 for men). The Central/Eastern Europe and Balkan region marries earlier and is more religious.

LGBTQ+ rights split along an east-west line. Bella's onboarding must be gender-neutral everywhere. Localized marketing should not assume same-sex couples can marry in IT, PL, RO, HU, SK, BG, LT, RS, UA or CZ.

### Cited Findings
- **Mean age of men at first marriage** (Eurostat 2021-22, via Landgeist/Milhaud) **[V]** — [Mapas Milhaud](https://mapasmilhaud.com/en/data-maps/average-age-of-european-men-at-first-marriage-2024/):
  - Highest: Sweden 37.5, Norway 36.9, Spain 36.9, France 36.0
  - Lowest: Ukraine 28.0
  - Full table — [Eurostat tps00014](https://db.nomics.world/Eurostat/tps00014)
- Over half of births occur outside marriage in FR, SE, NO, DK, SI, PT, BG, EE and NL, against a low share in GR, CY, HR, LT and PL **[bg]** — [Eurostat births outside marriage](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Fertility_statistics); [OECD Family Database SF3.3/SF3.1](https://www.oecd.org/els/family/database.htm)
- **ILGA-Europe Rainbow Map 2026** **[V]**:
  - Spain is #1, ending Malta's decade at the top (Malta drops to #2). Malta, Iceland, Belgium and Denmark are in the top five.
  - UK 44% (mid-table); Italy 36th; Cyprus 30th (33.69%)
  - Romania and Ukraine are in the low group
  - Sources — [PinkNews](https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/05/12/europe-lgbtq-2026-rainbow-map/); [ILGA-Europe](https://www.ilga-europe.org/report/rainbow-map-2026/); [Cyprus Mail](https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/05/12/cyprus-ranks-30th-in-europe-on-lgbt-rights-index)
- **Same-sex marriage legal** (year) **[bg]**: NL 2001, BE 2003, ES 2005, NO 2009, SE 2009, PT 2010, IS 2010, DK 2012, FR 2013, UK 2014/2020 (NI), LU 2015, IE 2015 (referendum), FI 2017, DE 2017, MT 2017, AT 2019, CH 2022, SI 2022, EE 2024, GR 2024 — [Wikipedia: Same-sex marriage in Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_union_legislation_in_Europe)
- **Civil union or partnership only** **[bg]**: IT 2016, HR 2014, CZ (enhanced partnership from 2025), CY 2015, LV (partnership from Jul 2024), HU (registered partnership only; 2025 constitutional amendment and Pride ban)
- **No recognition** **[bg]**: PL, RO, BG, SK, LT, RS, UA

### Inferences
- Use "partner" language by default. Offer "spouse", "fiancé(e)" and "sambo/PACSé(e)" as relationship-status options in SE/NO/DK and FR.
- In CEE and Southern markets, wedding-anniversary and religious name-day hooks resonate. In the Nordics, "dating anniversary" and Midsummer matter more.
- **Privacy expectations:** highest in DE, AT and CH (Datenschutz culture). Default to the minimum data shared between partners.

### Gaps

## 6. Language and address notes

### Takeaway
Bella should be informal with young couples nearly everywhere: du/tu/tú/je. Polite or formal forms persist in DE/AT/CH (Sie), FR (vous), IT (Lei), PL (Pan/Pani), CZ/SK (vykání), HU (Ön/maga) and Greek, but consumer apps in all these markets now commonly use informal address. Multilingual countries need several locales.

### Cited Findings (all **[bg]**)
- **Germany:** du is now standard in consumer apps (IKEA, Spotify, N26); Sie is safer for older users
- **Switzerland:** four national languages (de-CH: no ß, uses "ss"; fr-CH; it-CH; Romansh); Swiss number formats (CHF 1'234.50)
- **France:** tu is acceptable in a romantic or couple app. Loi Toubon requires French for consumer-facing terms and advertising — [Légifrance Loi 94-665](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/LEGITEXT000005616341)
- **Belgium:** FR, NL and DE; never ship nl-NL copy to Flanders (use nl-BE, with different vocabulary and tone)
- **Spain:** tú is standard; consider Catalan (Sant Jordi campaign), Basque and Galician locales
- **Italy:** tu in apps
- **Nordics:** du universally (Sweden's du-reformen); Norway has two written standards (Bokmål default, Nynorsk); Finland has a 5% Swedish-speaking minority
- **Ireland:** Irish (Gaeilge) as a goodwill touch only
- **Wales:** Welsh (Cymraeg) copy for Dwynwen
- **Luxembourg:** Luxembourgish, French and German
- **Ukraine:** Ukrainian only; avoid Russian
- **Baltics:** avoid Russian as default, but offer it in LV and EE where there are large Russian-speaking minorities. This is politically sensitive.
- **Serbia:** Cyrillic and Latin scripts are both used

### Inferences
- Give Bella a per-locale "register" setting: casual, warm by default.
- Date formats: DD.MM.YYYY (DACH, CEE, Nordics) or DD/MM/YYYY (UK, FR, IT, ES); 24-hour clock everywhere except casual UK; weeks start on Monday.

### Gaps
- No user research on preferred register for AI companions specifically.

## 7. Privacy and regulation beyond GDPR

### Takeaway
Italy's Garante is the most aggressive regulator against consumer AI chatbots: it banned ChatGPT in 2023, fined OpenAI €15M in Dec 2024 and fined Replika's maker €5M in 2025. It specifically targets age verification and legal basis.

At EU level, the AI Act's Article 50 transparency duties (telling users they are talking to an AI) apply from Aug 2026 **[bg]**. Bella must disclose that it is an AI in every channel.

### Inferences

- **Partner sharing:** a shared love journal involves both partners' data, and data about third parties (exes, family). Set up per-partner consent and private entries.
