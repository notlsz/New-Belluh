## 1. Dominant messaging channel per country (iMessage vs WhatsApp vs SMS/RCS vs Messenger)

### Takeaway
The Americas split in two. The US (and partly Canada) is a fragmented iMessage, SMS and RCS market with no dominant OTT app. Everywhere in Latin America, WhatsApp is the default at 73 to 92% reach. Bella should run on iMessage plus SMS/RCS in the US, Messenger plus SMS in Canada, and WhatsApp first everywhere else.

### Cited Findings
- **US:** Infobip platform data puts RCS at 80% reach, Instagram 55%, Apple Business Messages 50%, Messenger 45% and WhatsApp only 30%. Infobip calls the US "a unique market... as there is no dominant OTT messaging app." RCS volume grew 76x YoY in 2025. [V] — [Infobip Messaging Trends 2026](https://www.infobip.com/messaging-trends-report/regional-snapshot)
- **US teens:** 88% own an iPhone and 88% expect their next phone to be an iPhone (Piper Sandler, spring 2025, n=6,455). That means iMessage is the social default for young couples. [V] — [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/04/09/teen-iphone-ownership-continues-to-soar/)
- **Canada:** Messenger leads at 55%, then Instagram 50%, Apple Business Messages 50%, RCS 45% and WhatsApp 30% (Infobip platform data). [V] — [Infobip](https://www.infobip.com/messaging-trends-report/regional-snapshot)
- **Mexico:** WhatsApp 90%, RCS 50%, Instagram 45%, Messenger 45%, Telegram 25% (Infobip). [V] — [Infobip](https://www.infobip.com/messaging-trends-report/regional-snapshot)
- **Brazil:** WhatsApp 85%, RCS 70%, Instagram 65%. **Argentina:** WhatsApp 80%, Instagram 60%, Messenger 50%. **Colombia:** WhatsApp 75%, Messenger 50%, Instagram 40% (Infobip). [V] — [Infobip](https://www.infobip.com/messaging-trends-report/regional-snapshot)
- WhatsApp penetration of population: Brazil 92% (197M), Argentina 87%, Chile 85%, Peru 76%, Mexico 73%, Colombia 73%. The source cites no primary dataset, so treat these as indicative. [V, weak source] — [Aurora Inbox](https://www.aurorainbox.com/en/2026/03/05/whatsapp-business-latam-adoption/)
- Caveat: Infobip's "penetration" is measured on its own platform and is not a market-wide total. It differs from Aurora's figures (for example Mexico 90% vs 73%). [V] — [Infobip](https://www.infobip.com/messaging-trends-report/regional-snapshot)

### Inferences
- **US:** Make iMessage (Apple Messages for Business) plus SMS/RCS fallback the primary channel. WhatsApp is secondary, mostly for Hispanic and immigrant couples.
- **Canada:** Use SMS/RCS plus iMessage as the core, with Messenger as the social backup. WhatsApp matters for immigrant communities in Toronto and Vancouver.
- **All LatAm markets, including the briefer ones** (Uruguay, Costa Rica, Panama, DR, Ecuador, Guatemala, Paraguay, Bolivia, El Salvador, Honduras): WhatsApp is the primary channel. **[UNVERIFIED]** for each smaller market, since I found no per-country source, but WhatsApp is widely reported as the regional default.
- **Puerto Rico:** Likely a mixed iPhone/iMessage and WhatsApp market, given US carrier plans plus Latino social norms. **[UNVERIFIED]**
- **Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago:** Likely WhatsApp first. **[UNVERIFIED]**

### Gaps
- I found no reliable per-country iMessage share for the US or Canada (Apple does not publish it). DataReportal 2026 country reports were not fetched.
- I found no messaging data for Central America, the Caribbean, Bolivia or Paraguay.

## 2. Payments, subscription habits, price sensitivity, suggested Duo price (US anchor $29.99/mo)

### Takeaway
The US and Canada are card and App Store markets. In LatAm, instant account-to-account rails dominate: Pix in Brazil (with Pix Automático for recurring charges since June 2025), Bre-B in Colombia (launched Sept 2025), Yape and Plin in Peru, and Mercado Pago in Argentina and Mexico. Card-on-file subscriptions there have lower reach. Price at roughly 0.35 to 0.65x the US level.

### Cited Findings
- **Brazil:** Pix Automático went live June 16 2025. The user authorizes once with a maximum amount, and charges are then debited automatically. Use cases explicitly include subscriptions and streaming. Brazil has more than 160M Pix users and more than 800M keys. [V] — [Times Brasil](https://timesbrasil.com.br/brasil/economia-brasileira/banco-central-lanca-hoje-o-pix-automatico-entenda-como-funciona/)
- **Brazil:** Pix is reported at about 42% of online payments (headline only; the page failed to load). [UNVERIFIED, figure from headline] — [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-economia/pix-chega-a-42-dos-pagamentos-on-line-no-brasil/)
- **Colombia:** The Bre-B instant payment system from the central bank (Banco de la República) began operating in the second half of September 2025. Key registration opened July 14 2025. Transfers clear in under 20 seconds, 24/7, using cédula, phone or email as the key, and are integrated into Nequi, Daviplata, Bancolombia and BBVA apps. The initial cap is COP 10.95M per transfer. [V] — [Portafolio](https://www.portafolio.co/economia/bre-b-esta-es-la-fecha-oficial-en-que-comienzan-los-pagos-inmediatos-634750)
- **Peru:** Yape has about 14M users and Plin is the number two wallet. Digital wallets are displacing credit cards among Gen Z (May 2025). [V] — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2025/05/03/billeteras-digitales-como-yape-y-plin-estan-desplazando-a-las-tarjetas-de-credito-por-que/)

## 3. Romantic calendar: Valentine's equivalents, Mother's and Father's Days, anniversaries

### Takeaway
February 14 is not universal. Brazil peaks on June 12 and Colombia on the third Saturday of September (Sept 19 2026). Mother's Day dates scatter widely (May 10 fixed in Mexico, Guatemala and El Salvador; Aug 15 in Costa Rica; Dec 8 in Panama; third Sunday of October in Argentina; May 27 in Bolivia). Bella's occasion engine needs a per-country table, not a global default.

### Cited Findings

- **Mother's Day by country** [V] — [Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/2024/05/06/cuando-es-el-dia-de-la-madre-en-los-paises-de-latinoamerica/):

  - **Second Sunday of May:** Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Honduras, Peru, Puerto Rico, Uruguay
  - **Argentina:** third Sunday of October
  - **Bolivia:** May 27
  - **Costa Rica:** August 15
  - **Panama:** December 8
  - **Paraguay:** May 15
  - **Dominican Republic:** last Sunday of May
- US and Canada Mother's Day is the second Sunday of May. **[UNVERIFIED]** in this session, but standard. Jamaica and Trinidad and Tobago also likely use the second Sunday of May. **[UNVERIFIED]**
- **Father's Day by country** [V] — [Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/colombia/cuando-es-el-dia-del-padre-2025-en-los-paises-de-latam-mexico-colombia-y-mas/):

The company projected 14% order growth for 2025, an average ticket of R$215 to 220, and more than 800k orders a year. ## 4. Dating and couple culture: marriage age, cohabitation, religiosity, LGBTQ legal status

### Takeaway
People across the region marry late (around 28 to 35) or cohabit instead. Religion is declining fast but remains high in Brazil and Colombia. Marriage equality covers most large markets. The legal exceptions are Peru, Paraguay, the Dominican Republic, Central America (except Costa Rica and Panama, which has none) and the Caribbean (Jamaica criminalizes). Bella's copy and gender handling must be inclusive but low-key in those markets.

### Cited Findings
- **US:** Median age at first marriage in 2025 is 30.8 for men and 28.4 for women (23.5 and 21.1 in 1975). Married-couple households are 47% of all households (66% in 1975). 58% of 18 to 24 year olds live with their parents. [V] — [US Census CB25-TPS.78, Dec 2 2025](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2025/families-and-living-arrangements.html). A BGSU NCFMR profile covers cohabitation-before-marriage trends but was not fetched — [NCFMR](https://www.bgsu.edu/ncfmr/resources/data/family-profiles/FP-25-25.html)
- **Mexico:** INEGI recorded 486,645 marriages in 2024, a rate of 5.4 per 1,000 adults (6.9 in 2015). Average age at marriage is 32.1 for women and 35.0 for men. There were 6,312 same-sex marriages (3,879 between women, 2,433 between men) across 30 states. The bulletin has no data on unión libre. [V] — [INEGI EMAT 2024](https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/saladeprensa/boletines/2025/emat/emat2024_RR.pdf)
- **Religion (Pew, spring 2024 survey, published Jan 2026)** [V] — [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2026/01/21/catholicism-has-declined-in-latin-america-over-the-past-decade/):

  | Argentina | 58% | 24% | 37% |
  | Brazil | 46% (Protestant 29%) | 15% | 79% |
  | Chile | 46% | 33% | 40% |
  | Colombia | 60% | 23% | 57% |
  | Mexico | 67% (Protestant about 10%) | 20% | about half or more |
  | Peru | 67% | 12% | about half or more |
  Unaffiliated adults now outnumber Protestants in Argentina, Chile, Colombia and Mexico.
- **Same-sex marriage, legal (year):** Canada 2005, Argentina 2010, Brazil 2013, Uruguay 2013, US 2015, Colombia 2016, Ecuador 2019, Costa Rica 2020, Chile 2022, Mexico nationwide 2022. Bolivia recognizes "free unions" described as equivalent to marriage (officially from 2020, nationwide since 2023). [V, Wikipedia mirror with early-2025 snapshot] — [Recognition of same-sex unions in the Americas (mirror)](https://wiki.krisyotam.com/content/wikipedia_en_all_nopic_2026-03/Same-sex_marriage_in_North_America)
- **No recognition:**
  - Peru: a civil-union bill passed committee Nov 20 2024 but is not law
  - Panama, Guatemala and El Salvador: no recognition
  - Constitutional bans: Dominican Republic (2010), Paraguay (1992), Honduras (2005), Jamaica (2011)
  - Jamaica: same-sex activity is illegal
  - Trinidad and Tobago: listed as "illegal but not enforced"
  [V, same mirror]. Note: a 2024 High Court ruling in T&T struck down the buggery law, and an appeal was pending. **[UNVERIFIED in this session; status may have changed]**
- **Puerto Rico** follows US federal law, so same-sex marriage has been legal since 2015. **[UNVERIFIED, inferred from US status]**
- In January 2018 the Inter-American Court advisory opinion said its 20 member states should grant same-sex couples equal rights, including marriage. Implementation varies by country. [V] — [Q Costa Rica](https://qcostarica.com/inter-american-court-ruling-will-spread-marriage-equality-around-latin-america/)

### Inferences
- Bella should default to gender-neutral partner handling everywhere, with Spanish gendered forms chosen from the user's stated partner (novio/novia/pareja). Use "pareja" as the neutral default.
- In Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago, Honduras, Paraguay and the DR, avoid public LGBTQ-forward marketing for safety, but keep the product fully inclusive. Privacy features matter more there (discreet notifications, app lock).
- Family involvement: in LatAm, Mother's Day (May 10 in Mexico) and Father's Day are core gift moments, and partners are often expected to honor the other's parents. Bella should track in-law occasions. **[Inference, not sourced]**

## 7. Language and dialect notes; Quebec rules

### Takeaway
Ship at least six locales: en-US, en-CA, fr-CA (legally required for Quebec), es-MX (neutral LatAm Spanish base), es-AR (voseo, Rioplatense, also for Uruguay and Paraguay) and pt-BR. Optional variants are es-CO, es-CL and es-PE plus Caribbean English.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences (all **[UNVERIFIED]** linguistic norms, standard localization practice)
- **es-MX:** "tú"; "novio/novia"; "cita"; "¿qué onda?"; diminutives ("ahorita", "regalito"). It is the safest base for Central America and Peru.
- **es-AR/UY (Rioplatense):** voseo ("vos querés", "contame"); "che"; "salir con alguien"; "boliche". Plain "tú" reads foreign. Paraguay also uses voseo, with Guaraní code-switching ("jopara").
- **es-CO:** "usted" even between couples in parts of Colombia (Bogotá, Antioquia), plus "parcero" and "mi amor". Allow a formality toggle.
- **es-CL:** heavy slang ("pololo/polola" = boyfriend/girlfriend, "pololear" = to date). A Chilean Bella that says "pololo" will feel at home.
- **es-PE:** "enamorado/enamorada" = boyfriend/girlfriend.
- **es-DR/PR/Caribbean:** fast colloquial register; Puerto Rico is bilingual en/es with Spanglish norms.
- **pt-BR:** "você"; "namorado/namorada"; "ficar" (casual dating, distinct from namorar); "crush". Avoid European Portuguese entirely.
- **fr-CA:** Québécois terms: "chum" (boyfriend), "blonde" (girlfriend), "magasiner" (to shop), "courriel" (email), "fin de semaine" (weekend). Avoid France-French "weekend", "mail" and "shopping". The OQLF frowns on anglicisms.
- **Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago:** English UI with a warm Caribbean register. Avoid caricatured patois.
- Date and number formats: LatAm uses DD/MM/YYYY and the 24-hour clock is common; Brazil writes R$ 1.234,56; the US uses MM/DD and the 12-hour clock; French Canada uses 1 234,56 $.
