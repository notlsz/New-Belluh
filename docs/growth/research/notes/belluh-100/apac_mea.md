## Q1. Which messaging app should Bella live on in each country?

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is the default across South/Southeast Asia (outside the LINE and Zalo markets), the Gulf, Turkey and Africa. Five markets need a different channel: LINE (Japan, Taiwan, Thailand), KakaoTalk (Korea), WeChat (China), Zalo (Vietnam) and Messenger/Viber (Philippines). In Australia and NZ, survey data puts Messenger first, not iMessage. That contradicts the brief's assumption: iMessage/SMS is still the right channel for 1:1 conversations with a couple, but it is not the "most used app."

### Cited Findings
- GWI survey via DataReportal, as summarised by Sinch (Nov 2025, updated Mar 2026). Top app by country: Australia = Messenger, NZ = Messenger, Japan = LINE, South Korea = KakaoTalk, China = WeChat, Vietnam = Zalo, Philippines = Messenger (Viber also popular), Singapore/India/Indonesia/Malaysia/UAE/Turkey/South Africa/Nigeria/Kenya/Pakistan = WhatsApp. LINE is described as popular in Japan, Taiwan, Thailand and Indonesia. No percentages are given. — [Sinch, most popular messaging apps by country](https://sinch.com/blog/most-popular-messaging-apps-by-country/)
- Taiwan, Thailand, Hong Kong, Saudi Arabia, Israel, Qatar, Kuwait, Egypt, Morocco, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Kazakhstan, Bahrain, Oman, Jordan and Ghana are not in the Sinch/GWI table. — [Sinch](https://sinch.com/blog/most-popular-messaging-apps-by-country/)
- LINE: about 97M MAU in Japan, about 21M+ in Taiwan, about 55M in Thailand (LY Corp/LINE IR). [UV] — [LINE statistics roundup](https://expandedramblings.com/index.php/line-statistics)
- KakaoTalk: about 48–49M domestic MAU, effectively universal in Korea (Kakao IR). [UV] — [Kakao IR](https://www.kakaocorp.com/ir/main)
- WeChat/Weixin: about 1.4B MAU (Tencent quarterly results). [UV] — [Tencent IR](https://www.tencent.com/en-us/investors.html)
- Zalo: about 78M MAU in Vietnam (VNG). [UV] — [VNG / Zalo](https://zalo.me)
- Hong Kong: WhatsApp dominant, with WeChat for mainland contacts. Saudi/Gulf: WhatsApp plus very high Snapchat use. Israel: WhatsApp near-universal. Kazakhstan: WhatsApp and Telegram. Egypt/Morocco/Jordan/Ghana/Bangladesh/Sri Lanka: WhatsApp first, Messenger/IMO/Viber second. [UV, DataReportal Digital 2025 country reports are the place to confirm] — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports)

### Inferences
- Channel priority for Bella: WhatsApp Business API covers about 25 of the 33 countries. LINE Official Account plus Messaging API covers JP/TW/TH. Kakao Channel/Alimtalk covers KR. A WeChat Official Account or Mini Program covers CN and needs a Chinese entity. Zalo OA covers VN. Messenger plus Viber covers PH. iMessage (Apple Messages for Business) or SMS covers AU/NZ couples on iPhone.
- In the Gulf, keep WhatsApp but design for Snapchat sharing, such as shareable date-plan cards.

### Gaps
- No percentage shares were obtained this session. DataReportal's "Digital 2026" country pages (GWI "most used messaging platforms") are the source to pull for each country.

## Q2. Payments, subscription habits and a PPP-adjusted Duo price (US $29.99/mo)

### Takeaway
Cards and app-store billing work for AU/NZ/JP/KR/SG/HK/IL/Gulf. Everywhere else, local wallets and real-time QR rails decide conversion: UPI, GCash, PromptPay, QRIS, PayNow, M-Pesa, bKash, Kaspi and others. Gulf e-payments are near-total: Saudi Arabia reached 85% of retail payments in 2025. In much of the region, monthly prepaid passes beat auto-renewing subscriptions. Sticker prices need to drop to roughly 20–40% of the US price in South Asia, Africa and parts of SEA.

### Cited Findings
- Global Findex 2025: about 80% of adults worldwide hold an account. East Asia and Pacific: 83% have an account and 86% a smartphone. South Asia: nearly 80% have an account. India: 90% of adults have an account, but only 65% own a mobile phone. MENA: 53% account ownership (up from 45%). Sub-Saharan Africa: 58%, with the world's highest mobile-money use. 42% of adults in low- and middle-income countries made a digital merchant payment in 2024. — [World Bank press release, Jul 16 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/07/16/mobile-phone-technology-powers-saving-surge-in-developing-economies)

Electronic transactions rose from 12.6B to 14.6B, and SAMA credits growth in mada POS and e-commerce volumes. — [SAMA, Apr 2026](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/MediaCenter/News/pages/news-1139.aspx); [SAMA 2024](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-us/mediacenter/news/pages/news-1083.aspx)

## Q3. Romantic calendar by country

### Takeaway
Korea has the densest couple calendar in the world: monthly 14th "days," day-count anniversaries (100/200/300/1000 days) and Pepero Day. China has four big gifting peaks (Feb 14, 520, Qixi, and Single's Day as a shopping and anti-Valentine moment). Japan inverts Valentine's: women give, men return on White Day, and Christmas Eve is the couples' night. The Philippines counts monthsaries. In conservative Muslim markets, romance centres on engagement, marriage, Eid and wedding anniversaries rather than public dating holidays.

### Cited Findings
- Korea, 14th-of-month days: Jan 14 Diary Day, Feb 14 Valentine's (women give chocolate), Mar 14 White Day (men return candy), Apr 14 Black Day (singles eat jjajangmyeon), May 14 Rose Day, Jun 14 Kiss Day, Jul 14 Silver/Ring Day, Aug 14 Green/Music Day, Sep 14 Photo Day, Oct 14 Wine Day, Nov 14 Movie Day, Dec 14 Hug/Money Day. Also Pepero Day (Nov 11), 100-day and 1-year anniversaries, Christmas Eve. — [Korea Herald, Feb 2025](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10419651)
- Korea survey (Embrain, Nov 2024, n=1,000 aged 13–59):
  - Most important occasions: Pepero Day 80.7%, Valentine's 74.1%, White Day 68.9%.
  - Share seeing "day culture" as corporate-driven fell from 60.2% (2019) to 51% (2024).
  - Most active participants: college students (60.9%) and workers in their 20s (55.8%).
  — [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10419651)
- Korea day-count anniversaries (100, 200, 300 days, 1,000 days) are a widely documented couple custom. — [Daebak: 100 days culture](https://daebak.co/blogs/magazine/korean-couple-anniversaries-100-days-culture); [Fodor's: 14 romantic holidays](https://www.fodors.com/world/asia/south-korea/experiences/news/south-korea-celebrates-14-romantic-holidays-every-year-heres-the-full-calendar)
- China: Qixi falls on Aug 19 2026 and Aug 8 2027. — [ChinaTravel](https://www.chinatravel.com/culture/qixi-festival)
- China, other dates [UV]:
  - 520 (May 20): "wǔ èr líng" sounds like "I love you." Also 521. It is a peak date for gifting and marriage registration.
  - 1314 ("yīshēng yīshì," forever).
  - Single's Day (Nov 11) as a shopping and singles moment.
  - Feb 14 and Christmas Eve, with apples as gifts (píngguǒ/píng'ān).
- Japan [UV]:
  - Feb 14: women give honmei choco to partners and giri choco to colleagues; giri is declining.
  - Mar 14 White Day: men return gifts.
  - Christmas Eve is the couples' date night (restaurant and hotel bookings), while Christmas itself is not a family holiday.
  - Nov 22 "Ii Fūfu no Hi" (Good Couples Day) is a popular marriage-registration date.
  - Tanabata (Jul 7).
- Taiwan [UV]: Feb 14, White Day, 520, Qixi (major; flower prices spike), Christmas Eve.
- Hong Kong [UV]: Feb 14 (flowers essential, and prices triple), Qixi, Christmas Eve, Mid-Autumn.
- Singapore/Malaysia [UV]:
  - Feb 14; Chap Goh Mei (15th day of Lunar New Year, the "Chinese Valentine's" of Hokkien/Penang tradition).
  - Malaysia: JAKIM fatwa (2005) discourages Muslims from celebrating Valentine's. Muslim couples centre on Hari Raya.
- India [UV]:
  - Valentine's week (Rose Day Feb 7 through Valentine's Feb 14) is big in cities. There is backlash from some Hindu-nationalist groups.
  - Karva Chauth: wives fast for husbands; North India; October/November lunar date; 2026 date to confirm on timeanddate.
  - Teej; Raksha Bandhan (sibling, not couple).
  - Wedding season runs roughly Oct/Nov to Feb plus Apr to Jun, set by muhurat dates.
  - Anniversaries of arranged-marriage couples.
- Philippines [UV]: monthsary (monthly anniversary on the date the couple got together) is mainstream. Feb 14 sees mass weddings. Christmas season starts in September ("Ber months").
- Thailand [UV]: Feb 14 is very big, and couples register marriages at Bang Rak ("village of love") district office. Loy Krathong (November full moon) is a couples' night. Songkran.
- Indonesia [UV]: Valentine's is contested; some regions and clerics ban it, and Aceh restricts it. Romantic gifting peaks around Lebaran/Eid and weddings.
- Vietnam [UV]: Oct 20 Vietnamese Women's Day and Mar 8 International Women's Day are major flower and gift days for partners. Feb 14, White Day (Mar 14) and Qixi (Thất Tịch, "black bean dessert for singles") are also observed.
- Middle East/North Africa [UV]: public Valentine's was suppressed in Saudi Arabia until about 2018 and is now commercially visible. Romance is engagement- and marriage-centric: milka, katb al-kitab, wedding anniversaries, Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha gifting, Ramadan iftar dinners. Mother's Day (Mar 21 in Arab countries) is a large flower day.
- Israel [UV]: Tu B'Av (15 Av, July/August) is the Jewish love holiday. Feb 14 is secondary.
- Turkey [UV]: Feb 14 (Sevgililer Günü) is a major flower day (ÇiçekSepeti peak). Kandil nights.
- Australia/NZ [UV]: Feb 14; anniversary dinners; no distinct local couple holiday. Spring weddings fall September to November.
- South Africa/Nigeria/Kenya/Ghana [UV]: Feb 14 is very big (red clothing in Nigeria and Ghana). Ghana's Feb 14 doubles as National Chocolate Day. Christmas and Easter. Wedding introductions and traditional ceremonies (e.g., Ghana "knocking," Nigeria "introduction").

### Inferences
- Build Bella's occasion engine per locale:
  - KR: day counter (D+100, D+1000) and monthly 14th nudges.
  - PH: monthsary reminders.
  - JP: gender-aware Valentine's and White Day prompts, plus a Christmas Eve booking push in early November.
  - CN/TW/HK: 520, Qixi and Christmas Eve.
  - VN: Oct 20 and Mar 8.
  - IN: Karva Chauth and wedding-anniversary first.
  - MENA: wedding anniversary, Eid and Ramadan.
  - IL: Tu B'Av.

### Gaps
- Exact 2027 lunar dates (Karva Chauth, Loy Krathong, Eid, Tu B'Av) should be pulled from timeanddate.com. No survey data on spend per occasion outside Korea was collected.

## Q4. Couple culture: marriage, cohabitation, family, religion, LGBTQ legal status

### Takeaway
The East Asian markets (JP/KR/CN/TW/HK/SG) combine late marriage (about 30+), very low fertility and a long dating phase before marriage. That is ideal for a dating-couple product. South Asia, the Gulf, Egypt, Pakistan and Bangladesh are marriage-first, often arranged or family-brokered, so Belluh must be framed around engaged and married couples. LGBTQ status ranges from marriage equality (AU, NZ, TW, TH, ZA) to criminalization (much of the Gulf, Malaysia, South Asia ex-India, East and West Africa). Indonesia's new criminal code (in force 2026) criminalizes extramarital sex and cohabitation on complaint, which matters for any "unmarried couple" framing.

### Cited Findings
- 65 countries criminalise consensual same-sex acts per the ILGA World Database. The count rose in 2025 for the first time in almost a decade: Burkina Faso introduced criminalisation, and Trinidad and Tobago's decriminalisation was reversed. — [GCN, citing ILGA World](https://gcn.ie/countries-criminalising-same-sex-acts-2025/)
- Ghana: parliament passed the Human Sexual Rights and Family Values Bill on May 29 2026. It provides up to 3 years for identifying as LGBTQI+ and 3–5 years for "promotion." As of late June 2026 it awaited President Mahama's signature; a 2024 version was not signed. — [Urania Institute, Jun 29 2026](https://www.urania.institute/en/news/2026/0629-ghana-lgbtq-law-president-signature/); [Africanews, May 29 2026](https://www.africanews.com/2026/05/29/ghana-approves-sweeping-anti-lgbtq-law/). Status after June 2026 is unverified; one Ghanaweb headline reports Mahama deferring signature. — [Ghanaweb](https://mobile.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/NewsArchive/I-m-saddened-by-Mahama-s-excuse-to-defer-signing-anti-LGBTQ-bill-Paul-Yandoh-2028372)
- Kazakhstan: Tokayev signed a Russian-style ban on "LGBT propaganda" (reported Jan 2026). Same-sex acts are not criminalised. — [PinkNews, Jan 5 2026](https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/01/05/kazakhstan-lgbtq-propaganda-bill-law/); [Tengrinews](https://en.tengrinews.kz/laws_initiatives/Kazakhstan-bans-LGBT-propaganda-as-Tokayev-signs-law-270601)
- LGBTQ legal status by country [UV; confirm on the ILGA World Database, https://database.ilga.org]:
  - Same-sex marriage: Australia (2017), New Zealand (2013), Taiwan (2019), Thailand (in force Jan 2025), South Africa (2006).
  - Legal but no marriage: Japan (municipal partnership certificates; several high courts ruled the ban unconstitutional), Korea, China, Hong Kong (2023 CFA ordered a partnership framework and LegCo rejected the bill in 2025), Singapore (377A repealed in 2022; constitution amended to protect marriage definition), India (decriminalised 2018; no marriage per 2023 Supreme Court), Philippines, Vietnam, Israel (recognises foreign same-sex marriages), Turkey (legal; Pride banned; government hostility), Kazakhstan (propaganda law), Bahrain, Jordan, Indonesia (national law silent; Aceh criminalises; KUHP morality provisions).
  - Criminalised: Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Pakistan, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Egypt (de facto via "debauchery" laws), Morocco (Art. 489), Kenya, Nigeria (2014 SSMPA), Ghana (existing s.104 plus the new bill pending).
- Indonesia: the new Criminal Code (KUHP, Law 1/2023) took effect Jan 2 2026. It criminalises sex outside marriage and unmarried cohabitation, prosecutable only on complaint by a spouse, parent or child. [UV]
- Marriage and age patterns [UV; sources to confirm: national statistics offices, UN World Marriage Data]:
  - Mean age at first marriage is about 31 (men) / 30 (women) in Japan, about 34/31 in Korea, about 32/30 in Taiwan, Hong Kong and Singapore, and roughly 28–29 in urban China.
  - Japan, Korea and China report record-low marriages and very low fertility (Korea TFR about 0.72–0.75).
  - Cohabitation is high in AU/NZ (most couples cohabit before marriage) and low but rising in KR/JP.
  - India: arranged or "arranged-love" marriages remain the majority; family is central.
  - Pakistan, Bangladesh and the Gulf: marriage is family-brokered and dating is not public.
  - Philippines: no divorce law (annulment only). Nearly half of couples cohabit without marrying.
- Religion [UV]:
  - Majority Muslim: Indonesia, Malaysia, the Gulf, Turkey, Egypt, Morocco, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Jordan, Kazakhstan (secular state).
  - Majority Hindu: India. Majority Buddhist: Thailand, Sri Lanka. Majority Catholic: Philippines.
  - Majority Christian: Nigeria's south, Kenya, Ghana, South Africa.
  - Largely secular or irreligious: Japan, China, Korea (about half), Australia and NZ (no-religion pluralities).

### Inferences
- Default persona by market:
  - "Dating to married" for JP/KR/CN/TW/HK/SG/TH/PH/VN/AU/NZ/IL/ZA.
  - "Engaged/married" for IN/PK/BD/LK/ID/MY/Gulf/EG/MA/JO/KZ.
- In criminalising markets, ship same-sex couples without marketing it there. Do not store sexual-orientation fields in plaintext. Respect Apple/Google regional content rules. Avoid "unmarried couple" marketing in Indonesia.

### Gaps
- Exact current ages and rates per country were not fetched. Ghana's signature status after June 2026 and the Hong Kong partnership-bill outcome should be confirmed.

## Q7. Language, script and RTL notes

### Takeaway
RTL is needed for Arabic (Gulf, Egypt, Morocco, Jordan), Hebrew (Israel) and Urdu (Pakistan). CJK requires vertical-rhythm-safe fonts and honorific registers. Several markets need two languages: Malaysia (BM/EN/ZH), India (Hindi plus regional), Kazakhstan (Kazakh/Russian), Sri Lanka (Sinhala/Tamil), Hong Kong (Traditional Chinese with Cantonese colloquial).

### Cited Findings [all UV; standard locale facts]
- **Japanese:** keigo vs casual register. Bella should default to polite-casual (です/ます softened). Dates as 2026年11月1日. Era names (Reiwa 8 = 2026) appear in forms.
- **Korean:** 반말/존댓말. Couples use 반말, so Bella should use 해요체 by default and switch on opt-in. D-day format "D+100".
- **Chinese:** Simplified for CN/SG/MY; Traditional for TW/HK. HK colloquial Cantonese differs from TW Mandarin phrasing. Avoid 4 (死) and favour 520/1314/8 in prices and promos.
- **Thai:** no word spacing, so line-breaking needs ICU. Buddhist Era year (2569 BE = 2026 CE).
- **Vietnamese:** heavy diacritics, so check font coverage (Merriweather covers Vietnamese [UV]).
- **Bahasa Indonesia and Bahasa Melayu** are distinct and should not be shared.
- **Tagalog/Taglish:** English is fine for UI and Taglish for Bella's voice.
- **Hindi:** Hinglish voice; Devanagari plus regional scripts (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali). Use the Indian numbering format (lakh).
- **Arabic:** RTL; Gulf dialect vs Egyptian vs Darija (Morocco), with MSA for UI. Hijri dates alongside Gregorian. Week starts Sunday in IL and some Gulf states, Saturday or Monday elsewhere. The UAE weekend is Sat–Sun (since 2022); KSA/Qatar/Kuwait weekend is Fri–Sat.
- **Hebrew:** RTL, gendered verbs (address partners by gender), Shabbat-aware booking (Fri evening–Sat).
- **Turkish:** dotted/dotless i casing bug.
- **Urdu:** RTL Nastaliq. **Bengali, Sinhala, Kazakh** (Cyrillic, with a Latin transition planned).
- Merriweather has no Arabic, Hebrew, CJK, Thai or Devanagari glyphs, so fallback pairings are needed (e.g., Noto Serif JP/KR/SC/TC, Noto Naskh Arabic, Frank Ruhl Libre for Hebrew). [UV]

### Inferences
- Mirror the layout in RTL, including the sidebar and floating bar. Keep emoji non-mirrored.

### Gaps
- No user-research data on preferred Bella register per market.
