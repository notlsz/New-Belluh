## Cross-market data: trust in AI, payments and regulation baselines

### Takeaway
Emerging markets (India, Indonesia, Thailand, Mexico, Turkey, Gulf, Nigeria) are markedly more excited about and trusting of AI than the Anglosphere and core EU; the US and UK are the most nervous large markets. Regulation is diverging: EU (risk-based, now delayed), Korea/Vietnam/Italy (national AI acts in force), China (licensing + labeling), US (no federal law), Brazil (bill stalled).

### Cited Findings
**Ipsos AI Monitor 2025** (30 countries, 23,216 online adults, fieldwork 21 Mar–4 Apr 2025; China, Philippines, Saudi, UAE, Nigeria not surveyed) — [Ipsos AI Monitor 2025 PDF](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2025-06/Ipsos-AI-Monitor-2025_0.pdf). - **Pew (published 15 Oct 2025, 25 countries):** median 34% more concerned than excited, 42% equally, 16% more excited; no country had "more excited" as the largest group; roughly half in the US, Italy, Brazil, Australia, Greece are more concerned than excited; UK women 47% vs men 32% more concerned — [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2025/10/15/concern-and-excitement-about-ai/). Full per-country tables: [Pew report PDF](https://alpha.pewresearch.org/pewresearch-org/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/2025/10/pg_2025.10.15_ai_report.pdf) (not fetched).
- **KPMG / University of Melbourne "Trust, attitudes and use of AI: A global study 2025":** 48,340 people, 47 countries; 66% use AI regularly; ~80% in emerging economies use AI regularly/semi-regularly vs <70% in most advanced economies; only ~2 in 5 in advanced economies willing to trust AI; 65% trust AI's technical ability but only 52% its safety/security; 56% of employees admit mistakes from unchecked AI output; 70% of frequent work users use free public tools, only 18% pay for public AI tools — [HR Online summary](https://humanresourcesonline.net/new-global-study-reveals-high-public-adoption-of-ai-across-47-countries); full report on [University of Melbourne figshare](https://figshare.unimelb.edu.au/articles/report/Trust_attitudes_and_use_of_artificial_intelligence_A_global_study_2025/28822919).
- KPMG 2025 country groupings: UAE 91% and Saudi 88% overall AI use; India 91% use at work and 81% trust at work; Nigeria, China, Egypt, Turkey grouped with trust "often above 80%"; Finland, Netherlands, Sweden, Canada, Germany below 50% trust; Japan, Germany, UK work use below 60% — [People Matters ME](https://me.peoplemattersglobal.com/news/technology/gulf-nations-lead-in-ai-trust-adoption-and-digital-literacy-heres-what-the-data-tells-us-45405).
- **World Bank Global Findex 2025:** 79% of adults globally have an account (74% in 2021); 75% in low/middle-income economies; 61% of adults in LMICs made/received a digital payment; 42% paid merchants digitally; mobile money accounts 40% of adults in Sub-Saharan Africa (27% in 2021) and 37% in Latin America (22%); 86% own a mobile phone; 1.3bn adults still unbanked — [World Bank blog](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/allaboutfinance/digital-technology-is-unlocking-financial-inclusion).
- **EU AI Act after the AI Omnibus** (Parliament adopted 16 Jun 2026, Council 29 Jun 2026, OJ 24 Jul 2026): Art. 5 prohibitions since 2 Feb 2025; GPAI obligations since 2 Aug 2025 (pre-existing models by 2 Aug 2027); Art. 50 transparency from 2 Aug 2026 (50(2) synthetic-content marking for GPAI postponed to 2 Dec 2026); Annex III high-risk moved to 2 Dec 2027; Annex I high-risk to 2 Aug 2028; new ban on CSAM/non-consensual intimate imagery generators from 2 Dec 2026 — [Future of Privacy Forum](https://fpf.org/blog/the-ai-act-implementation-timeline-what-changes-under-the-ai-omnibus).
- **Erin Meyer frameworks** (used per market below): 8 scales (communicating low-/high-context, evaluating direct/indirect negative feedback, persuading, leading, deciding consensual/top-down, trusting task-/relationship-based, disagreeing, scheduling) — [HBR "Navigating the Cultural Minefield" 2014](https://hbr.org/2014/05/navigating-the-cultural-minefield)†; decision-making and negotiation by country — [HBR "Getting to Si, Ja, Oui, Hai, and Da" 2015](https://hbr.org/2015/12/getting-to-si-ja-oui-hai-and-da)†.

### Inferences
- "Excitement" and "nervousness" are not opposites: India and Thailand score high on both; Japan is low on both (low salience). Messaging should be benefit-led in high-excitement markets and control/safety-led in US/UK/France.
- The KPMG finding that only 18% of frequent work users pay for public AI tools suggests bottom-up consumer monetization is hard everywhere; enterprise/team plans and bundling with existing paid channels (telcos, super-apps, cloud marketplaces) carry the revenue.

### Gaps
- KPMG per-country "willing to trust" and Pew per-country concern figures were not extractable; the full PDFs above should be consulted.
- Edelman Trust Barometer 2025/2026 AI-specific country data was not retrieved this session.
- No single source gave SaaS price levels by country; PPP-based pricing advice below is inferential.

## United States

### Takeaway
Largest, highest-WTP software market with the most nervous public; buyers expect self-serve trials, ROI proof, SOC 2, and fast procurement; regulation is a state-law patchwork.

### Cited Findings
- Channels: no single super-app; B2B discovery via Google search, LinkedIn, G2, product-led trials (inference; see Gaps). Messaging split between SMS/iMessage and others rather than WhatsApp — [Sinch messaging apps by country](https://sinch.com/?p=74525) (not fetched).
- Trust: Ipsos excited 38 / nervous 64 / trust-not-discriminate 37 — among the most nervous of 30 countries — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2025-06/Ipsos-AI-Monitor-2025_0.pdf); roughly half of US adults more concerned than excited — [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2025/10/15/concern-and-excitement-about-ai/); KPMG reports US/UK regular use at 52% — [People Matters ME](https://me.peoplemattersglobal.com/news/technology/gulf-nations-lead-in-ai-trust-adoption-and-digital-literacy-heres-what-the-data-tells-us-45405).
- Culture: Meyer places the US as the most low-context, task-based-trust culture; decisions fast and individually owned but frequently revisited — [HBR 2015](https://hbr.org/2015/12/getting-to-si-ja-oui-hai-and-da)†. Hofstede: very high individualism, low power distance †.
- Regulation: no federal AI statute; California SB 53 (Transparency in Frontier AI Act) signed 29 Sep 2025 †; Colorado AI Act effective date pushed to 30 Jun 2026 †; a December 2025 executive order sought to preempt/challenge state AI laws † — [IAPP global AI law tracker](https://iapp.org/news/a/global-ai-law-and-policy-tracker-highlights-and-takeaways) (not fetched). Sector rules (HIPAA, GLBA, FTC Act §5) apply †.
- Competitors: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft Copilot, Salesforce Agentforce, plus vertical AI (Harvey, Sierra, Decagon, Glean) † (general market knowledge).

### Inferences
- What wins: (1) PLG + credit-card self-serve with a 14-day path to value, then enterprise sales for expansion; (2) security/compliance artifacts (SOC 2 Type II, SSO, DPA) at launch; (3) measurable outcome pricing (per resolution/seat) because buyers are skeptical of AI hype given low public trust.
- Price at full list; the US is the anchor price that other markets discount from.

### Gaps
- No 2025-26 DataReportal US figures retrieved; no US-specific B2B buying-cycle data (e.g., Gartner buying-group size) fetched.

## China

### Takeaway
A closed ecosystem: WeChat/Alipay/DingTalk/Feishu distribution, strict CAC licensing and labeling, and fierce, cheap domestic models; Western AI firms effectively cannot serve consumers directly.

### Cited Findings
- Channels: WeChat (Weixin) ~1.4bn MAU; enterprise collaboration via WeCom, DingTalk (Alibaba), Feishu/Lark (ByteDance) — [Tencent investor relations](https://www.tencent.com/en-us/investors.html)†.
- Payments: Alipay and WeChat Pay duopoly for mobile payments †; Findex shows near-universal account ownership in China † — [Findex 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/globalfindex)†.
- Trust: KPMG groups China among countries with trust often above 80% — [People Matters ME](https://me.peoplemattersglobal.com/news/technology/gulf-nations-lead-in-ai-trust-adoption-and-digital-literacy-heres-what-the-data-tells-us-45405). China not in Ipsos 2025 sample — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2025-06/Ipsos-AI-Monitor-2025_0.pdf).
- Regulation: AI Content Labelling Measures issued 7 Mar 2025, effective 1 Sep 2025 (explicit labels + embedded metadata for synthetic content); Anthropomorphic (AI companion) Services Measures effective 15 Jul 2026; Cybersecurity Law amendments (Jan 2026) raise penalties and broaden extraterritorial enforcement — [Latham & Watkins, Sep 2026](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/2026/09/ai-regulation-in-apac-diverging-approaches-across-the-region). Interim Measures for Generative AI Services effective 15 Aug 2023 (filing/security assessment for public-facing services) †; PIPL effective 1 Nov 2021 (data localization, cross-border transfer assessments) †.
- Culture: Meyer: high-context, relationship-based trust (guanxi), top-down decisions — [HBR 2014](https://hbr.org/2014/05/navigating-the-cultural-minefield)†. Hofstede: high power distance (80), low individualism (20), very long-term orientation †.
- Competitors: DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen, ByteDance Doubao, Moonshot Kimi, Zhipu, Baidu Ernie †.
- Past entrants: Uber China sold to Didi (Aug 2016) †; Amazon closed its domestic China marketplace (2019) †; LinkedIn shut its China social network (2021) and InCareer (2023) † — [Reuters on LinkedIn](https://www.reuters.com/technology/microsoft-shut-down-linkedin-china-2021-10-14/)†.

### Inferences
- What wins: (1) a local JV/partner holding licenses and operating inside WeChat/DingTalk/Feishu mini-programs; (2) serving Chinese companies going abroad (出海) from outside China is more viable than serving inside China; (3) price competitiveness against near-free domestic models (DeepSeek-driven price wars) — compete on workflow and compliance, not model access.
- For US-headquartered AI firms, export-control and CAC exposure make China an "adjacent" market (Chinese brands entering other markets), not a direct one.

### Gaps
- No 2025-26 data on Chinese enterprise SaaS willingness to pay was retrieved; widely reported preference for customized/on-prem deployment could not be cited.

## Japan

### Takeaway
High-trust in relationships but slow, consensus-driven buying (ringi); LINE and PayPay are the consumer rails; the AI law is light-touch; partners (SIers) and Japanese-language polish decide deals.

### Cited Findings
- Channels: LINE is the dominant messenger (LY Corporation) — [LY Corp strategy](https://www.lycorp.co.jp/integrated-report/en/strategy/strategy.html).
- Payments: PayPay registered users surpassed 75 million (roughly 3 in 4 Japanese) — [Payment Navi](https://paymentnavi.com/?p=180136); METI announced 2024 cashless ratio crossed the 40% target (42.8%) — [METI press release 31 Mar 2025](https://www.meti.go.jp/english/press/2025/0331_001.html) (figure from prior knowledge †; 2023 was 39.3% per [Impress Watch](https://www.watch.impress.co.jp/img/ipw/docs/1651/061/html/cash_2024_02_o.jpg.html)).
- Trust: Ipsos excited 46 / nervous 29 / understand AI just 41 — lowest understanding in the sample — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2025-06/Ipsos-AI-Monitor-2025_0.pdf); KPMG: work use below 60% — [People Matters ME](https://me.peoplemattersglobal.com/news/technology/gulf-nations-lead-in-ai-trust-adoption-and-digital-literacy-heres-what-the-data-tells-us-45405).
- Regulation: Japan is a voluntary, principles-based jurisdiction — [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/2026/09/ai-regulation-in-apac-diverging-approaches-across-the-region); AI Promotion Act (Act on Promotion of R&D and Utilization of AI-Related Technologies) enacted May 2025 † — [OECD.AI](https://oecd.ai/en/dashboards/policy-initiatives/act-on-promotion-of-research,-development,-and-utilization-of-artificial-intelligence-related-technologies). APPI governs personal data †.
- Culture: Meyer: most high-context culture; consensual decisions (ringi/nemawashi) that are slow but stick once made — [HBR 2015](https://hbr.org/2015/12/getting-to-si-ja-oui-hai-and-da)†. Hofstede: uncertainty avoidance 92, masculinity 95 †.
- Competitors: NEC, Fujitsu, NTT (tsuzumi), Preferred Networks, Sakana AI; SoftBank–OpenAI "SB OpenAI Japan" JV (2025) †.
- Past entrants: eBay exited Japan in 2002, losing to Yahoo! Auctions †.

### Inferences
- What wins: (1) go through system integrators/resellers (NTT Data, Fujitsu, NEC, SoftBank) who own the trust relationship; (2) native-quality Japanese UI, docs and support, plus case studies from named Japanese firms (social proof reduces uncertainty avoidance); (3) pilot (PoC) → departmental → company-wide sequencing, priced for invoice/bank transfer as well as card.
- Low public understanding + low nervousness means education-heavy marketing works better than fear-of-missing-out.

### Gaps
- DataReportal 2026 LINE MAU figure and Japanese SaaS ARPU benchmarks not retrieved.

## South Korea

### Takeaway
Hyper-digital, fast-adopting, nationalistic about platforms (Kakao, Naver); the AI Basic Act is in force since Jan 2026 with a local-representative rule.

### Cited Findings
- Channels: KakaoTalk is the default messenger; Naver competes with Google in search † — [wmtips Korea messaging](https://www.wmtips.com/technologies/instant-messaging/country/kr/) (not fetched).
- Trust: Ipsos excited 69 / nervous 40 / understand 70 — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2025-06/Ipsos-AI-Monitor-2025_0.pdf); Pew: 39% of Koreans who heard a lot about AI are more excited than concerned vs 19% of those who heard a little — [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2025/10/15/concern-and-excitement-about-ai/).
- Regulation: AI Framework (Basic) Act effective 22 Jan 2026; high-impact AI duties (impact assessments, risk management, explanation, human oversight, user notice); labeling of generative outputs; foreign providers must designate a domestic representative if prior-year revenue > KRW 1tn, AI revenue > KRW 10bn, or >1m daily Korean users; 2026 pilot year with one-year penalty grace — [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/2026/09/ai-regulation-in-apac-diverging-approaches-across-the-region). PIPA is one of Asia's strictest privacy laws †.
- Culture: Meyer: high-context, hierarchical, top-down decisions, relationship trust (often built after hours) †. Hofstede: power distance 60, individualism 18, uncertainty avoidance 85 †.
- Competitors: Naver HyperCLOVA X, LG AI Research Exaone, Upstage (Solar), SK Telecom, Kakao (Kanana; OpenAI partnership Feb 2025) †.
- Past entrants: Yahoo exited Korea 2012 after losing to Naver †; Walmart and Carrefour exited 2006 †.

### Inferences
- What wins: (1) partner with a chaebol or Kakao/Naver for distribution (OpenAI–Kakao is the template); (2) Korean-language quality and integration with Kakao login/KakaoPay/Naver Pay; (3) speed — Korean users churn fast if releases lag the US.

### Gaps
- Claim that Korea is ChatGPT's second-largest paid market (reported 2025) not verified this session.

## India

### Takeaway
Massive, AI-enthusiastic, WhatsApp- and UPI-native market with low per-user willingness to pay; win with freemium, sachet/regional pricing, Indic languages and telco bundles; DPDP rules phasing in through 2027.

### Cited Findings
- Payments: UPI processed a record 21.63bn transactions worth ₹27.97trn in December 2025 — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/news/upi-ends-2025-strong-note-record-transactions-december-126010100687_1.html); over 22,000 crore (220bn) UPI transactions in calendar 2025 — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/upi-logs-another-record-year-transactions-cross-22000-cr-mark-in-2025); UPI share of digital payment volume ~83.4–83.7% in FY25 (sources differ slightly) — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/upi-s-contribution-to-payments-ecosystem-volume-grows-to-83-4-in-fy25-125052900871_1.html); [Financial Express via Magzter](https://www.magzter.com/hi/stories/newspaper/Financial-Express-Pune/UPI-SHARE-IN-DIGITAL-PAYMENTS-ECOSYSTEM-SWELLS-TO-837).
- Channels: WhatsApp is the primary messaging and business channel † (Meta reports India as its largest WhatsApp market).
- Trust: Ipsos excited 65 / nervous 65 / trust-not-discriminate 67 — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2025-06/Ipsos-AI-Monitor-2025_0.pdf); KPMG: 91% use AI at work, 81% trust it at work — [People Matters ME](https://me.peoplemattersglobal.com/news/technology/gulf-nations-lead-in-ai-trust-adoption-and-digital-literacy-heres-what-the-data-tells-us-45405); Ipsos India: 65% trust government to regulate AI responsibly — [Apeejay News](https://apeejay.news/65-indians-trust-the-government-to-regulate-ai-responsibly/?amp=1).
- Regulation: DPDP Rules notified Nov 2025, staggered 18-month rollout — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/digital/breaking-dpdp-act-goes-live-with-phased-implementation-over-next-18-months-84212.htm); [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/indias-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023-brought-into-force-). IT Intermediary Rules amendments effective 20 Feb 2026: prominent labels on AI-generated content, provenance metadata, user declarations for platforms with >5m users, 2-hour takedown for deepfake NCII and 3 hours for other illegal content — [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/2026/09/ai-regulation-in-apac-diverging-approaches-across-the-region).
- Culture: Meyer: high-context, hierarchical, relationship-based trust, flexible scheduling †. Hofstede: power distance 77 †.
- Competitors: Sarvam AI, Krutrim (Ola), Reliance Jio (with Google Gemini/Meta partnerships), Zoho, Freshworks †. OpenAI launched low-priced ChatGPT Go in India (Aug 2025) †.
- Past entrants: Walmart acquired Flipkart (2018) rather than build †; Google Pay and PhonePe dominate UPI app share while WhatsApp Pay remained small †.

### Inferences
- What wins: (1) free tier + very low INR price points (₹199–₹499/month range for prosumer) or telco bundles; (2) Hindi + regional languages and WhatsApp-first UX; (3) UPI AutoPay mandates for recurring billing (cards are a minority rail).
- India is also a talent/support base and a launchpad for Indian SaaS selling globally.

### Gaps
- India-specific PPP software price benchmarks and WhatsApp user count from DataReportal 2026 not retrieved.

## Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines, Thailand)

### Takeaway
Four different markets: Indonesia and Thailand are the most AI-excited in the Ipsos sample; each has its own messenger (WhatsApp / Zalo / Messenger / LINE) and its own national QR rail; super-apps (Grab, GoTo) and e-wallets (GCash, MoMo) are the distribution layer.

### Cited Findings
- Trust: Indonesia excited 80 / nervous 43 / understand 91 (highest); Thailand excited 79 / nervous 58 / trust-not-discriminate 78 — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2025-06/Ipsos-AI-Monitor-2025_0.pdf). Philippines and Vietnam not in Ipsos 2025.
- Channels: Indonesia WhatsApp; Vietnam Zalo; Philippines Facebook Messenger; Thailand LINE † — [wmtips Thailand](https://www.wmtips.com/technologies/instant-messaging/country/th/) (not fetched).
- Payments: national QR standards QRIS (Indonesia), VietQR, PromptPay (Thailand), QR Ph; e-wallets GCash/Maya (PH), MoMo (VN), GoPay/OVO/DANA (ID) †. Half of Filipinos aged 15+ now own bank accounts per Findex 2025 — [Malaya Business Insight](https://malaya.com.ph/business/business-news/half-of-filipinos-in-15-age-group-now-own-bank-accounts-wb).
- Regulation: Vietnam AI Law No. 134/2025/QH15 (enacted 1 Mar 2026; high-risk systems must comply by 1 Mar 2027; Decree 142/2026/ND-CP) — [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/2026/09/ai-regulation-in-apac-diverging-approaches-across-the-region). Indonesia PDP Law (Law 27/2022) fully applicable Oct 2024 †; Vietnam PDP Law effective 1 Jan 2026 †; Thailand PDPA fully in force 1 Jun 2022 †; Philippines Data Privacy Act 2012 †.
- Culture: Hofstede: Indonesia power distance 78, very collectivist; Thailand collectivist with high uncertainty avoidance; Philippines power distance 94 †. Meyer: high-context, relationship-based, hierarchical decisions across the region †.
- Competitors: GoTo/Indosat "Sahabat-AI" (Indonesian LLM), AI Singapore SEA-LION, SCB 10X Typhoon (Thai), FPT AI, Viettel AI, VinAI (Vietnam), Grab's in-house AI †.
- Past entrants: Uber sold its SEA business to Grab (Mar 2018) †; Shopee overtook Lazada via mobile-first, free shipping and gamified local marketing †.

### Inferences
- What wins: (1) distribute through a super-app or e-wallet partner (Grab, GoTo, GCash, LINE) rather than standalone app stores; (2) chat-commerce/agent products inside WhatsApp/Zalo/LINE/Messenger; (3) price in local currency with e-wallet and QR payment, not card-only.
- Treat Vietnam as the regulatory first-mover (AI law) and Indonesia as the volume play.

### Gaps
- No 2025-26 sourced MAU figures for Zalo, GCash or LINE Thailand; no SEA SaaS ARPU data.

## Germany

### Takeaway
Skeptical, privacy- and process-driven buyers; cash and invoice culture; works councils can veto employee-monitoring AI; data sovereignty (EU hosting) is table stakes.

### Cited Findings
- Trust: Ipsos excited 45 / nervous 46 — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2025-06/Ipsos-AI-Monitor-2025_0.pdf); KPMG: Germany among countries with trust below 50% and work use below 60% — [People Matters ME](https://me.peoplemattersglobal.com/news/technology/gulf-nations-lead-in-ai-trust-adoption-and-digital-literacy-heres-what-the-data-tells-us-45405).
- Payments: cash still ~half of point-of-sale transactions (Bundesbank 2023 payment behaviour study: 51%) † — [Deutsche Bundesbank](https://www.bundesbank.de/en/publications/reports/studies)†; B2B pays by SEPA invoice; PayPal is the top online wallet †.
- Regulation: EU AI Act timeline as above — [FPF](https://fpf.org/blog/the-ai-act-implementation-timeline-what-changes-under-the-ai-omnibus); GDPR + BDSG; works council co-determination over technical systems able to monitor employees (BetrVG §87(1) no. 6) †.
- Culture: Meyer: low-context, very direct negative feedback, task-based trust, consensual decisions, strict scheduling — [HBR 2015](https://hbr.org/2015/12/getting-to-si-ja-oui-hai-and-da)†. Hofstede: uncertainty avoidance 65, long-term orientation 83 †.
- Competitors: SAP (Joule), DeepL, Aleph Alpha, Black Forest Labs, Parloa, Helsing, n8n †.
- Past entrants: Walmart exited Germany in 2006 after cultural missteps (e.g., mandated greeting/smiling, union conflict) †.

### Inferences
- What wins: (1) EU data residency, German-language DPAs, ISO 27001/C5 attestation; (2) engage the works council early for any HR/employee-facing AI; (3) detailed technical documentation and references from Mittelstand peers — long cycles, but sticky.

### Gaps
- No 2025-26 German B2B SaaS buying-cycle data retrieved.

Hofstede: power distance 68, uncertainty avoidance 86 †.

### Inferences
- What wins: (1) French-first product and contracts, French hosting; (2) present the conceptual model and governance before the demo; (3) position as complementary to Mistral/European sovereignty or partner with French SIs (Capgemini, Sopra Steria).

## Italy

### Takeaway
SME- and family-business-dominated, relationship-driven, WhatsApp-heavy; the Garante is Europe's most aggressive AI enforcer; first national AI law in the EU.

### Cited Findings
