## Plan

Do not launch in mainland China. Google Play, WhatsApp, Telegram and LINE are unavailable; WeChat needs a local entity, ICP licence and PIPL localisation; the CAC human-like interaction measures (in force Jul 15 2026) require 2-hour reminders, a minor mode, algorithm filing and security assessments. Serve Chinese-speaking couples abroad (SG, MY, US, CA, AU) with the existing zh UI and the CN kit instead.

See [strategy.md](../strategy.md), "Excluded and restricted markets".
